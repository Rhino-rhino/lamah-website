      Questions

      What does an end of tenancy clean include?

      Kitchen (oven inside, hob, extractor, cupboards, behind and under appliances), bathrooms descaled and scrubbed, living areas and bedrooms (skirting boards, switches, inside window sills and frames, wardrobes), hallways and floors. Carpet cleaning is a separate service.

      What is the 72-hour re-clean guarantee?

      If a cleaning issue is raised within 72 hours of your clean, we return to put it right.

      What if the property is very dirty?

      Our prices assume standard condition. Heavily soiled properties are quoted separately. Send photos with your enquiry and we can usually tell you upfront.

      Do you charge for carpet cleaning?

      Yes, as an add-on at £55 per room. Carpet cleaning booked on its own has a £100 minimum call-out charge.

      Which areas do you cover?

      All of London.
