      Frequently asked questions

      What does an end-of-tenancy clean include?

      Everything required for checkout: full kitchen degreasing (oven interior, racks, hob, extractor hood, fridge/freezer, behind and under appliances, inside all cupboards), full bathroom descaling, bedrooms and living areas (skirting boards, switches, doors, internal windows/sills, wardrobes), hallways, and floors. Carpet steam cleaning can be added for £55 per room.

      How does the 72-hour re-clean guarantee work?

      If you, your landlord, or your inventory clerk spot any missed cleaning detail within 72 hours of completion, send us the report or photos and our team will return free of charge to put it right.

      Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?

      Yes. Our teams arrive fully equipped with commercial-grade vacuums, steam cleaners, ladders, microfibre cloths, and professional descalers and degreasers.

      What if the property hasn't been cleaned in a long time?

      Our standard price guide covers properties in normal condition. If a property is heavily soiled or requires extreme limescale/grease removal, upload photos in the quote form below and we will send a fair, fixed written quote before you book.

      Which parts of London do you cover?

      We cover all London boroughs across Zones 1–6, seven days a week.
